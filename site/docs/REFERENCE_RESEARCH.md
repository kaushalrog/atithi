# Reference research

Inspected on **2026-10-21** in the Claude desktop app's built-in browser, at a 1440 × 900 viewport. For each site I read the page text and took a screenshot of the first screen, except where noted. "Observed" is what was on the page. "Lesson" and "Don't copy" are my interpretation.

Cookie banners were left untouched, so they appear in some screenshots. Nothing was signed into.

## Lessons carried into the design

1. **The product is the hero, but not a call button.** Vapi and ElevenLabs lead with "call the agent". Atithi leads with what happens *after* the words: the booking record. That's the Granola move, applied to receptionist work.
2. **Never gate the first screen on animation** (family.co). Text and controls render at first paint; the Welcome Signal draws in *behind* readable content.
3. **No borrowed trust.** Every competitor uses logos, quotes or outcome numbers. Atithi has none, so the trust section is about what the owner can *inspect* (transcripts, records, the promise check), plus an honest status for each channel.
4. **Supporting elements recede** (Linear). Navigation small and quiet; one accent colour; borders soft and few.
5. **No microphone on page load, no phone number required** (Vapi, Retell). The sample booking is scripted, labelled, and runs entirely in the page.
